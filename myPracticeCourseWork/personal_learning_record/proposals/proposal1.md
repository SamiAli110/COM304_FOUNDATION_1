## Explanation of this proposal
Hot desking means that employees are not permanently assigned desks or offices. Instead, work stations are treated as shared, flexible resources that can be occupied by any employee temporarily. This depends on availability, or through a digital booking or reservation system.

In organizational and ICT contexts, hot desking is considered a spatial efficiency model used to optimise the physical environment. This seeks to address the problem of underused workplaces, which means office spaces are not being utilized. This is extremely common in modern hybrid or mobile workforces where employees do not remain in a fixed workstation throughout the day. As a result, organizations can reduce the total number of required desks relative to the number of staff. This principle is often referred to as "desk sharing ratios".
Hot desking is supported by information systems such as:
Desk booking platforms
Digital check-in check-out system
IoT sensors monitoring desk availability 
Cloud-based user profiles allow staff to log into any workstation
